Common Heating Problems We Solve

Commonly caused by dirty flame sensors, cracked hot-surface silicon nitride igniters, or tripped high-limit safety switches due to clogged air filters. Our trucks carry universal replacement igniters and control boards for instant same-day repair.
Banging at ignition suggests delayed ignition where gas builds up before lighting. Squealing indicates a failing ECM blower motor bearing or slipping blower belt. We perform precise vibration diagnostics to prevent complete motor seizure.
Short cycling creates tremendous wear on gas valves and control boards. It is usually caused by restricted airflow causing the heat exchanger to overheat, or a faulty anticipator setting on an outdated thermostat.

Air Conditioning Diagnostics & Tune-Ups
