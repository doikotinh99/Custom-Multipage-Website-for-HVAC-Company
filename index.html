Chicago Deep Freeze Checklist

Our Chicago NATE technicians inspect 28 critical furnace touchpoints: infrared combustion analysis, burner orifice cleaning, manifold gas pressure calibration, and ultrasonic heat exchanger crack inspection to prevent dangerous carbon monoxide leaks.
High-efficiency 90%+ condensing furnaces produce acidic condensate. In negative Illinois wind chills, exterior PVC drain terminations freeze solid, tripping furnace safety shutoffs. We install specialized insulated pipe heating wraps and freeze-resistant trap systems.
A dual-fuel system pairs an electric inverter heat pump with a high-efficiency gas furnace. The smart controller automatically calculates real-time electric vs. natural gas rates in Illinois and switches heating modes seamlessly at the 30°F economic balance point!

Summer Heatwave & AC Protection

Humid Midwest air causes evaporator coils to collect dirt and biological film. Our chemical-free foaming bath dissolves baked-on grime, restoring optimal heat transfer and lowering your monthly cooling electrical consumption by up to 22%.
Summer electrical storms across Cook and Will counties trigger power dips that burn out expensive AC compressors. We install commercial-grade compressor soft-start kits that reduce initial starting torque by 70%, extending compressor lifespan by years.
Best Comfort is a registered Trade Ally. When upgrading to qualifying 16+ SEER2 cooling or cold-climate heat pumps, we file all utility rebate paperwork for you—securing instant credits up to $2,000 directly off your invoice!
