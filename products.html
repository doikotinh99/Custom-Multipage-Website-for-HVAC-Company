Equipment Buying & Sizing FAQ

How we calculate the right tonnage, AFUE rating, and ductwork compatibility for your home.

An oversized unit will short-cycle, spiking electric bills and failing to dehumidify Chicago’s sticky summer air. An undersized unit will run constantly on sub-zero winter nights without reaching your thermostat setpoint. We calculate precise Manual J load formulas considering window orientation, insulation R-values, and square footage.
As of 2023, the U.S. Department of Energy enacted SEER2 (Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio 2). SEER2 tests equipment under realistic higher static duct pressures (0.5" w.g. vs 0.1" w.g.), providing an accurate reflection of how your AC performs in a real home duct system.
